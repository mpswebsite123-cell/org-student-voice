About this demo

What this workspace demonstrates

Case filtering, report review, status changes and internal reviewer notes using fictional sample information.

What it does not do

  • It is not an official government or school portal.
  • It does not forward reports to any real agency.
  • It does not verify identities or guarantee anonymity.
  • It is not monitored for emergencies.

If a real person is at immediate risk, contact a trusted adult or the appropriate local emergency service rather than using this demo.
